Home — Q4: what goes in the floating card?

Route detail has a floating card top-left that collapses into a pill when you scroll the sheet. Home gets the same slot — the question is only what is written in it. Three candidates, same board, same map, same collapse. Scroll the list inside any phone (or drag its grab handle) to collapse the card; tap a pill to open it again. Everything else on these three screens is identical on purpose.

One difference from Route detail worth noticing: Home is a tab root, so there is no back lens for the card to tuck behind. Settings is the floating lens, and it sits top-right — so Home's card collapses by shrinking from the right rather than stepping sideways. Simpler motion than the route card's, and it needs no left transition at all.

What to look for

  • Which pill do you want to look at while scrolling? That is the whole question — the expanded state is only on screen for the first second.
  • Try "Last known, 6 min ago". That is (a)'s strongest argument: today it is a grey subtitle under the Nearby title that nobody reads, and on a ranked Home it is the sentence that explains why the order looks odd. Watch where it goes in (b) — nowhere. It would need to live in the sheet.
  • Try "Tsim Sha Tsui East". Route detail's round-4 finding was that HK names barely collapse; a locality is shorter than a destination, so (a) actually makes a real pill where the route card struggles to.
  • Scroll (b) slowly past a section boundary. The pill changing under your thumb is either a helpful signal or a twitch — that is not decidable on paper.
  • (c) is the lean. It is also one more moving part, and the cost shows up when you scroll back to the top: the card has to swap contents and grow.

Not being asked here

  • The board's content, ranking and section names — that is §5 of the proposal and is settled enough to draw.
  • The sheet's detents, the map or the tab bar. All three are Route detail's, unchanged.
  • Whether the ETA figures are right. They are hand-written here; the real ones come from homeView.
