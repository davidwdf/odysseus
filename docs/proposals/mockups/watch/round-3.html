Round 3 — the pill shares the row, and what the second line should say

Three answers to round 2's feedback. The lens shares the row and the pill rises above it to open at full width — the owner's fix, and it is better than stacking. “2 stops away” is dropped: HK stop spacing runs from 1 to 10 minutes, so a stop count is not a time and reads as one. And the podcasts-style dock (§2) is measured rather than guessed, because it is the layout that decides how much the pill can say. Phones are 390 px, an iPhone 15's CSS width, so the truncation here is the truncation you would get.
