Watch a stop — five pills, three cards

A rider on Route detail opens a stop's sheet and taps Watch. From then on the route-at-stop follows them around the app as a floating element above the tab bar, and a tap expands it. These are the shapes that element could take, with the real bar geometry underneath (54 tall, 12 inset, 24 radius) so the crowding is honest. Everything here is invented data; the arms are the real ones.

Board says

1 — The pill

What sits there while you browse. Tap any of them to expand.

2 — The card

What the pill becomes. Independent of the choice above — any pill can open any card.

Deliberately wrong here and not to be copied: raw hex instead of @nextbus/ui tokens, English-only strings, a countdown that never ticks because there is no data behind it, and a max-height expansion (the real one animates a measured height). The arms, their order and the geometry are the parts meant to be judged.
